## Observable being compared

One fixed irrational `alpha/pi`, first N complete circular-billiard chords, geometric Euclidean tube **half-width** `c/N`, disk-area vacancy `V_N(c)`. Proposed contribution: a radial dephasing identity reducing the two-branch spatial vacancy to the square of the one-branch rotation-gap vacancy, then explicit unequal subsequential **spatial area** limits. Its proof and novelty remain subject to the project's audit gates. Neither the central caustic, the three-gap theorem, fixed-rotation nonconvergence in general, nor a theorem's exclusion of this parameter regime establishes novelty.

## Direct geometry and illumination comparisons

1. **Tabachnikov, _Billiards_.** [Historical book URL](https://num.math.uni-goettingen.de/~summer/tabachnikov_billiardsbook.pdf). **Access: 404 on this audit.** Earlier conversation cited this for classical circular caustics; no page-level verification here. The elementary chord calculation independently gives supporting-line distance `cos(alpha)`; do not label that new. Obtain a stable author/publisher edition and exact pages before manuscript citation.

2. **Altmann, Friedrich, Motter, Kantz, Richter (2008), _Prevalence of marginally unstable periodic orbits in chaotic billiards_.** [Primary arXiv text, Section II](https://arxiv.org/html/0711.4585#S2). **Checked text:** Section II describes coprime star polygons `(q,eta)`, eccentric circular scatterers avoided by those orbits, inner and outer arrangements, mixed arrangements in Fig. 3, and geometric conditions (1)–(3). This is a strong overlap with the rejected “largest disk avoiding one complete periodic star” direction. No exact match to our proposed largest-disk formula was established. Do not substitute the maximal jump into a displayed inequality without checking its arrangement and angle conventions. No shrinking-width finite-prefix spatial area theorem was located in this inspected section.

3. **Dettmann and Fain (2017), _Linear and nonlinear stability of periodic orbits in annular billiards_.** [Primary arXiv text](https://arxiv.org/html/1609.03652). **Checked text:** Section III, equations (3)–(6), bounds inner-obstacle radii, records circular caustics, and constructs an orbit hitting the inner scatterer and reversing along the polygon. Related obstacle-clearance geometry; its modified colliding trajectory differs from preserving every segment of our original orbit. This comparison does not establish novelty of an empty-disk formula.

4. **Chernov and Galperin (2003), _Search light in billiard tables_, Regular and Chaotic Dynamics 8(2), 225–241.** [Primary journal page and abstract](https://rcd.ics.org.ru/RD2003v008n02ABEH000239/); [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1070/RD2003v008n02ABEH000239); [historical author PDF](https://people.cas.uab.edu/~mosya/papers/ILLM-RCD.pdf). **Verified journal abstract; full proof not audited.** The journal describes eventual illumination from a cone of initial directions, including circle/ellipse regions. This differs from a finite segment union around one fixed-angle orbit. DOI access failed through the web tool; author PDF appeared in search indexing, but direct PDF availability was not established in this audit. Do not treat a search-index excerpt as a retrieved full paper.

5. **Pilaud and Santos (2009), _Multitriangulations as Complexes of Star Polygons_.** [Accessible poster](https://page.math.tu-berlin.de/~felsner/Lehre/SemFlips/Poster/Multitri-Star-M.B.pdf). **Checked poster, not full article.** Combinatorial multitriangulations/star terminology is relevant to naming the deliberately chosen odd dense family. It supplies no inspected empty-area or tube-coverage result. Even vertex counts can also form stars, e.g. `(8,3)`.

6. **Aurenhammer (1991), _Voronoi Diagrams—A Survey of a Fundamental Geometric Data Structure_.** [Accessible survey PDF](https://people.math.sc.edu/Burkardt/classes/urop_2016/aurenhammer.pdf). **Title, abstract, survey scope checked; relevant optimization sections not audited.** General Voronoi machinery is prior background for largest-empty-circle methods. Exact segment-site and disk-boundary specialization remains a literature task, not a new algorithm claim.

## Rotation gaps, hitting times, and known arithmetic phenomena

7. **van Ravenstein (1988), _The Three Gap Theorem (Steinhaus Conjecture)_.** [Publisher/DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S1446788700031062). **Publisher landing page checked; full proof not audited.** Classical exact rotation-gap arithmetic is prior machinery. The golden-ratio gap constants in this project need direct derivation and numerical checks independently of this citation.

8. **Coelho and de Faria (1996), _Limit laws of entrance times for homeomorphisms of the circle_, Israel Journal of Mathematics 93, 93–112.** [Publisher abstract](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF02761095). **Abstract checked; subscription full text not read.** Studies entrance point processes into descending renormalization intervals for a fixed irrational rotation number. It characterizes convergence through subsequences of the Gauss map/natural extension. Therefore fixed-rotation subsequential limit laws are established prior territory. No claim here that its precise theorems immediately imply or exclude our spatial identity.

9. **Coelho (2004), _The loss of tightness of time distributions for homeomorphisms of the circle_, Transactions of the AMS 356(11), 4427–4445.** [Author's institutional record/abstract](https://pure.york.ac.uk/portal/en/publications/the-loss-of-tightness-of-time-distributions-for-homeomorphisms-of/); [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-04-03386-0). **Institutional abstract checked; full proof not read.** Discusses subsequential limits of rescaled interval hitting-time processes and tightness versus bounded type. Its abstract explicitly reports natural-scale nonconvergence. This materially weakens any broad claim that arithmetic nonconvergence itself is new. The candidate must isolate the spatial area transformation.

10. **Dettmann, Marklof, Strömbergsson (2017), _Universal hitting time statistics for integrable flows_.** [Primary arXiv text](https://arxiv.org/html/1606.04068). **Checked text:** Section 2, equation (2.11), definition of `f`-regularity, Theorem 1; opening scope and billiard examples. The theorem assumes an absolutely continuous parameter measure and an absolutely continuous frequency-direction pushforward, plus target genericity/transversality. A single fixed frequency direction fails that `f`-regularity condition. Hence this theorem cannot simply be cited as already proving the fixed-angle claim. That hypothesis mismatch is not evidence of originality; its surrounding literature and more singular extensions still require comparison.

11. **Polanco, Schultz, Zaharescu (2015 preprint), _Continuous Distributions Arising from the Three Gap Theorem_.** [Primary arXiv text](https://arxiv.org/html/1511.09399). **Checked text:** abstract, introduction, equation (1.1), Theorem 1.1, and section headings for joint spacings. Studies gap distributions averaged over the rotation parameter using Farey arithmetic; neighboring joint spacings are also treated. Supplies related gap machinery, rather than an inspected fixed-angle disk tube-area identity. Do not infer independence of two spatial branches from one-gap frequencies alone.

12. **Zhao, _Almost sure convergence of cover times for psi-mixing systems_ (2024 preprint, revised 2025).** [Primary arXiv text](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.17425). **Checked text:** Section 5, Definitions 5.1–5.3 and Theorem 5.4. Rotation cover-time growth can have distinct liminf/limsup exponents controlled by Diophantine type. The displayed theorem there assumes type greater than one, so it does not by itself give our golden bounded-type area oscillation. Cover time and normalized spatial uncovered area are distinct observables. Continued fractions causing unusual coverage behavior are established background.

13. **Bunimovich and Dettmann, _A Billiard in an Open Circle and Riemann Zeta Function_.** [Publisher DOI page](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10586458.2024.2423180). **Access: 403; historical comparison only.** Earlier conversation characterized the observable as survival through boundary holes. That description was not newly verified from full text here. Obtain an accessible author version before making a detailed theorem comparison; boundary survival need not equal spatial chord-tube vacancy.

## Literature gates before claiming a new theorem

1. Read full Coelho–de Faria and Coelho proofs. Translate our branch-vacancy observable into their hitting-time/point-process language; test whether radial averaging is a routine corollary.
2. Follow backward and forward citations from those papers, van Ravenstein, and Dettmann–Marklof–Strömbergsson. Search fixed-frequency two-target laws, integrated correlations, and deterministic shrinking neighborhoods.
3. Audit Minkowski/parallel-set volumes of finite orbit segments, deterministic coverage, geometric discrepancy, and convex polygon approximation. Our fixed-positive-width polygon formula may already be standard.
4. Retrieve stable Tabachnikov and open-circle versions; resolve Altmann arrangement conventions. Cite exact theorem/page support, not search-match absence.
5. Give an independent specialist the exact candidate statement and proof. Record whether known results imply it, which lemma is genuinely additional, and any counterexample or missing uniformity.
6. Only after proof and comparison gates pass, formulate a narrow originality claim with a dated search scope. Publication suitability and peer review remain external judgments.
